Bodyweight & Calisthenics

Master Your Bodyweight

We are one of the few gyms in the city with a dedicated calisthenics setup. Whether you want to learn your first pull-up or master the muscle-up, this area is for you.

Setup Includes:

  • - Multi-grip pull-up bars
  • - Parallel dip bars (high and low)
  • - Gymnastic rings
  • - Plyometric boxes
  • - Resistance bands for assisted training

Mobility Guide

We also provide foam rollers, yoga mats, and PVC pipes in this area for your warm-up and cool-down mobility sessions.
