Gym Amenities

Changing Rooms

Separate changing rooms for men and women with daily use lockers. Please bring your own lock if you want to leave your belongings.

Shower Area

Clean and hygienic shower stalls with hot and cold water available during gym hours.

Water Dispenser

RO purified drinking water available for free. Please bring your own sipper or bottle to avoid plastic waste.

Parking

Ample free parking space available for both two-wheelers and four-wheelers right outside the gym building.
