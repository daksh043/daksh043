About Us

Gym History

PulseFit Gym was started in 2018 with a single room and basic free weights. Today, we have expanded into a full sports hub providing dedicated areas for strength training, cardio, and calisthenics. We focus on providing quality equipment and a friendly environment for our members.

Owner's Note

"I wanted to build a gym that is affordable but doesn't compromise on equipment quality. Whether you want to lose weight or build muscle, our doors are open for everyone." - Owner, PulseFit Gym.

Gym Etiquette & Rules

  • 1. Bring your own towel and water bottle.
  • 2. Re-rack your weights after using them.
  • 3. Wear proper sports shoes. No sandals allowed on the gym floor.
  • 4. Wipe down machines after use.
  • 5. Do not drop heavy dumbbells on the floor.
