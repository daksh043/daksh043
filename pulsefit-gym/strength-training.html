Strength Zone

Heavy Duty Equipment

Our strength section is built for people who are serious about lifting. We have a wide range of free weights and heavy-duty machines.

Available Equipment:

  • - Dumbbells ranging from 2.5kg to 50kg
  • - 3 Olympic Squat Racks
  • - 2 Flat Bench Press setups, 1 Incline, 1 Decline
  • - Leg Press Machine, Hack Squat Machine
  • - Cable Cross Over station

Powerlifting Friendly

We allow chalk and heavy deadlifts. We have dedicated deadlift platforms with bumper plates so you can train safely without worrying about damaging the floor.
